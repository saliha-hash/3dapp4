Reading & calculation

Understand how the final measurement is obtained⌄

The scale is read in steps of 0.05 mm. The final result is the observed reading after applying zero correction.

Final reading = MSR + (VC × LC) − zero error
Here, 1 main-scale division = 1 mm and 20 Vernier divisions = 19 mm. Therefore LC = 1.00 − 0.95 = 0.05 mm.

How it works

Least count and measurement notes⌄
Least count = 1 MSD − 1 VSD
In this simulation, 1 MSD = 1 mm and 1 VSD = 0.95 mm, so the least count is 0.05 mm.
Final reading = observed reading − zero error
The input dimensions define the example object's true size. The displayed reading is the simulated caliper measurement rounded to its least-count resolution—not a separate physical measurement of a real object.
The supplied 3D model is preserved. The magnified scale diagram is a teaching aid for the reading calculation.
